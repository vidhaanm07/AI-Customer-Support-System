# AI-Customer-Support-Automation-System

📌 Overview

SupportFlow AI is an autonomous AI-powered customer support automation system that streamlines the complete customer service lifecycle using n8n workflow automation and Large Language Models (LLMs).

The system automatically receives customer queries, analyzes their intent, classifies tickets, assigns them to the appropriate support agent, generates AI-powered responses, tracks resolution, and collects customer feedback.

By combining AI agents, workflow automation, and intelligent decision-making, SupportFlow AI reduces manual workload, improves response time, and enables scalable customer support operations.

🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional customer support systems face several challenges:

Manual ticket classification
Slow response times
Incorrect ticket routing
High workload on support teams
Lack of automated follow-up
Limited customer insights

Support teams spend significant time performing repetitive tasks instead of solving complex customer issues.

💡 Solution

SupportFlow AI creates an end-to-end autonomous support pipeline where AI agents handle repetitive support operations.

The system can:

✅ Understand customer queries
✅ Classify support tickets
✅ Detect sentiment and priority
✅ Automatically assign agents
✅ Generate personalized responses
✅ Track ticket resolution
✅ Collect customer feedback
